# Final limitations

- Two exploratory pair-boundary tasks cannot establish universal generalization.
- This is a research classification evaluation, not clinical validation.
- External tasks are not full five-class evaluations.
- Others_proxy is not semantically identical to internal Others.
- No consistent external specialist superiority.
- tau=0.10 was frozen on validation in Phase 9.
- No equivalence or non-inferiority margin was tested.
- Lexical and error analyses are descriptive/observational.

- Development, locked-test, SDCNL, and Depression Reddit metrics remain separate and are never pooled.
- Seed-level summaries are repeated-model analyses over shared evaluation rows, not independent datasets.
